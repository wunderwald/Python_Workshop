# Data

## `0001_fried_ts.tsv` / `0001_fried_codebook.xlsx`

Experience sampling (ESM) data from 79 Dutch university students, 4 prompts per day
for 14 days, starting 16 March 2020 (the first days of the COVID-19 lockdown).
Items are rated 1–5; see the codebook for exact wording and scales.

- **Source:** Fried, E. I., Papanikolaou, F., & Epskamp, S. (2022). Mental health and
  social contact during the COVID-19 pandemic: An ecological momentary assessment study.
  *Clinical Psychological Science, 10*(2), 340–354. https://doi.org/10.1177/21677026211017839
- **Distributed via:** [openESM](https://openesmdata.org), dataset `0001`, version 3.0.0
  (Zenodo DOI [10.5281/zenodo.17347269](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17347269))
- **License:** CC-BY 4.0

A copy is kept here so the workshop notebooks load fast and reliably from GitHub,
independent of Zenodo availability.
